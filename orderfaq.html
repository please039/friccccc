Orders FAQs

Here are some frequently asked questions about orders:

  • How can I track my order? You can track your order by logging into your account and checking the order status.
  • What is the return policy? We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Please refer to our return policy page for more details.
  • Can I change my shipping address after placing an order? Unfortunately, we cannot change the shipping address once the order has been placed. Please ensure your address is correct before confirming your order.
  • How long does it take to process an order? Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your order has been shipped.
ORDER ISSUES

If you encounter any issues with your order, please contact us as soon as possible:

  • Missing items: If any items are missing from your order, please contact our support team with your order number and details of the missing items.
  • Damaged items: If you receive damaged items, please take photos of the damage and contact our support team for assistance.
  • Incorrect items: If you receive incorrect items, please contact our support team with your order number and details of the incorrect items.
